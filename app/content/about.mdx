Hi, I'm **Arvind Kumar**. I'm an amateur nature photographer, and this website is specifically dedicated towards bird photography.

<br />

This hobby is important to me since it combines two individual skills:
- Identifying birds, and predicting their behavior.
- Photographing small, distant, fast moving subjects that appear briefly.

It's always a treat to capture a new bird you haven't seen before, or to add a '[lifer](https://support.birda.org/en/articles/5604264-what-is-a-lifer)' in birdwatching terms. \
I go birding most Sundays in Bengaluru, India. \
Check out [my eBird profile](https://ebird.org/profile/MTA2NTk5ODM) for more info.

Gear: Sony α7 III + Tamron 150-500mm F5-6.7 handheld.
